Interpellation no. 380: questions to the Government raised in 2018

MP Zuzana Majerová Zahradníková asked Prime Minister Andrej Babiš to address a Cannabis is The Cure complaint and inaction by the Government Office and the Ministries of Justice, Interior and Health. The Government's reply contains a related letter from the Government Office.

  1. Original parliamentary question (2 pages) — 17 December 2018; parliamentary receipt stamp 19 December 2018
  2. Original Prime Minister reply and enclosures (3 pages) — 41872/2018-KPV-2; 39287/2018-OVL; 41180/2018-OVL

The Government Office letter is dated 20 December 2018; the conversion certificate is dated 14 January 2019. Neither establishes the date of the Prime Minister's own response, which is not reliably legible in the supplied copy.
