CANNAINSIDER NEWS · 27 JULY 2026 · FRANZ KAFKA TODAY · REPORT 27072026-008

The Czech orb-weaver from Branibor has been spinning a web along Prague–Brno–Prague and back for more than fifteen years. Who will untangle it?

Prague identified Brno as competent. Brno returned the original part of the filing and three supplements to Prague. In the meantime, Prague’s supervisory review ended on a file that, according to the supervising authority itself, lacked decisive records. Five records dated 27 July expanded the map with forensic, review, substantive, supervisory and connecting branches.

The “spider” is an editorial metaphor for the documented referral, division, return and supervision of filings. It does not designate an individual and is not evidence of illegality, a criminal offence or anyone’s liability. What the records establish are dates, case references, quoted official statements and an unresolved question: which authority will take responsibility for a complete substantive review.

Prague sent a branch to Brno. Brno sent it back

On 11 June 2026, the Prague Municipal Public Prosecutor divided the filing of 27 May and forwarded the part concerning the conduct of the Supreme Public Prosecutor to its Brno counterpart. Brno recorded it under 3 ZN 140/2026 but returned it to Prague on 21 July.

“This part was returned to the Prague Municipal Public Prosecutor […] on the ground of substantive competence, including all three […] attachments and supplements.”

Brno Municipal Public Prosecutor, 21 July 2026, 3 ZN 140/2026-3 (Czech PDF)

The record proves the return and the reason stated for it. It does not determine which legal view is correct. It does establish the contradiction: each office identified the other as competent.

Supervision over an incomplete file

The Prague High Public Prosecutor closed the supervisory review as unfounded on 23 July. The same record described the underlying Prague file in these terms:

“It does not contain […] the NSZ communication of 13 May 2026 […] or your filings of 25 April, 8 May and 12 May 2026.”

Prague High Public Prosecutor, 23 July 2026, 1 VZN 1678/2026-70 (Czech PDF)

This does not automatically establish that the outcome was unlawful. It does establish that the file lacked the listed records. The later return of the complete Brno branch with three supplements therefore changes the factual basis on which the earlier review proceeded.

Five records in one day

Five published copies are dated 27 July 2026. They establish the contents and addressees of the author’s requests; they do not by themselves establish delivery or the correctness of the legal positions:

  1. Regional Police / OKTE Frýdek-Místek, copied to the District Public Prosecutor: a request for existing SOPs and methodology concerning THC measurements in 2009–2019 (Czech PDF);
  2. Brno Regional Public Prosecutor: a request to review the reasoning, completeness and handling of the jurisdictional conflict (Czech PDF);
  3. Prague Municipal Public Prosecutor: a demand either to take and decide the returned matter or to submit the conflict for a decision without another referral loop (Czech PDF);
  4. Prague High Public Prosecutor: a new fact supporting renewed supervision because the Brno branch was not taken for a substantive decision (Czech PDF);
  5. Supreme Public Prosecutor: the urgent supplement connected the three prosecution-service branches at their common superior authority.

The fifth record addressed to the Supreme Public Prosecutor says:

“This is not a new isolated filing intended for another automatic referral.”

Urgent supplement to the NSZ pre-action demand, 27 July 2026 (Czech PDF, 5 pages)

One day under the author’s stated timetable

The supplement says that the demand was delivered on 14 July and that the author’s fourteen-day period ends on Tuesday, 28 July 2026. On the author’s calculation, one day remained on 27 July. This report does not treat that period as an automatically applicable statutory deadline; it records the timetable stated in the pre-action demand.

Who should untangle the web?

  1. Did Prague take the returned branch, or submit the conflict to the common superior authority?
  2. Which three records came back from Brno, and where are they now recorded?
  3. Which reference and unit hold the police and NCOZ branch?
  4. Will Prague High Public Prosecutor renew supervision in light of the new fact?
  5. Will Brno Regional Public Prosecutor review the reasoning, notice and completeness of Brno’s handling?
  6. Will the Supreme Public Prosecutor identify one responsible authority and prevent another Prague–Brno referral loop?

What the records establish—and what they do not

  • Established: the listed filings, references, referrals, return, review steps and exact statements in the linked PDFs.
  • The author’s allegation: that these steps form part of a fifteen-year pattern and require one substantive review of the methodological, forensic and human-rights issues.
  • Not established by referral alone: guilt, criminal conduct, intent, the correctness of every legal argument, or the final outcome of any proceeding.
  • Next step: a reviewable decision identifying competence, file completeness, the responsible authority and the procedural outcome of every branch.
