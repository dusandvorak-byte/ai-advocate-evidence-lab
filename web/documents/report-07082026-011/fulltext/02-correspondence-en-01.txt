Dear Mr Ballotta,

I hope this message finds you well.



I am writing to follow up on the materials that were delivered on Monday, 25 May 2026, at the
reception of the European Union Drugs Agency (EUDA) in Lisbon, to your attention, in accordance
with your kind instructions of 22 May 2026.

The delivered materials included in particular:

    •   a letter addressed to Dr Lorraine Nolan concerning the current situation of Czech cannabis
        policy and legislation,

    •   materials of the Czech alliance of non-governmental organisations Cannabis is The Cure,

    •   information concerning the criminalisation of cannabis research, education and patients in
        the Czech Republic,

    •   documentation relating to the methodology used for THC measurement and its implications
        in criminal proceedings,

    •   and, most importantly, our submission dated 18 May 2026 requesting EUDA's attention to
        issues concerning cannabis analysis methodologies, legal certainty, public health and human
        rights implications in the Czech Republic.

Could you kindly confirm whether these materials were successfully received and forwarded to the
relevant persons within EUDA?



Furthermore, we would be grateful if you could indicate when we might reasonably expect a
response from EUDA, particularly regarding our submission of 18 May 2026. In that document, we
raised concerns regarding the methodology used for determining THC content in cannabis, possible
discrepancies with internationally recognised scientific standards and guidance, and the broader
consequences of these practices for patients, scientific research and public health.



We fully understand that such matters require careful consideration. Nevertheless, after many years
of unsuccessful efforts to obtain meaningful examination of these issues at the national level, any
indication of the expected timeframe for EUDA's response would be greatly appreciated.
