The score measures connection, urgency and review need only. It does not determine guilt, guarantee reopening, or replace a lawyer.

What relevance means to an outside reader

9/9: direct match with a case, institution or evidence node requiring priority review. 7–8/9: strongly supplements chronology or argument, but the procedural relationship must be checked. 3–6/9: the same legal or evidentiary problem without a demonstrated direct link. 1–2/9: general thematic connection only. 0/0: no recognised match with this memory, not a claim that the document is irrelevant elsewhere.

What the platform does and what we are building Open a concise view of current features and planned development.

Available today

Reports, sources and safe matching

  • Document-based reports with direct links to published source records.
  • A chronological archive and search by title, institution or case reference.
  • Local checking of a selected record or a public PDF at a direct HTTPS link: an explicitly supported sample can be recognised, while an unknown record never inherits another document’s legal conclusions.
  • Tentative procedural-map placement with separate source facts, interpretation, uncertainty and proposed solutions. Every displayed item carries an exact quotation from the record.
  • A visible separation of source, editorial summary, relevance and limitations, with agreed anonymisation of other individuals.

Added value in development

Long-term evidence memory

  • Show what a new record changes compared with the position before and after its delivery.
  • Compare each new record with the complete versioned evidence network and safely store human-confirmed links.
  • Add OCR and voice input for faster document processing.
  • Enable secure sharing and tailored outputs for the public, journalists and legal professionals.
